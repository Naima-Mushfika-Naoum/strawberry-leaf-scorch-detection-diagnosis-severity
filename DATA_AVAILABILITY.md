# Data availability

The source images originate from the publicly available [PlantifyDr dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lavaman151/plantifydr-dataset). The study selected and curated strawberry images and created additional annotations; the public source and study-specific subsets are distinct resources.

This distribution provides code and pretrained models. It does not contain curated images, annotations, split records, or extracted feature tables.

The curated and annotated subsets used in the published study may be requested by emailing **naoum1496@gmai.com**. Please identify the paper, the subset or annotations needed, and the intended research use. Availability is considered upon reasonable request; this repository does not promise automatic access.

The paper's Data Availability section states that the curated and annotated subsets (Datasets 1–4) are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s44196-026-01458-x

Use the source dataset's attribution and applicable terms when obtaining images. This repository does not grant rights to third-party images.
